# Objective

Decide how OpenBot lands on T3 Code.

The Objective is complete when:

- The mapping of Bot, Channel, Chat, Session, Home and Workspace onto T3
  threads, projects and provider instances is recorded in `CONTEXT.md`,
  including what group and Bot-to-Bot Channels need that T3 lacks.
- The Computer runs as a right-panel tab in the fork, proven live, with its
  upstream edit count measured.

Standalone OpenBot keeps working until the T3 mode replaces it; new standalone
v1 features wait for this decision.
